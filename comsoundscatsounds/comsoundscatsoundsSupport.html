Frequently asked questions

Where do the cat sounds come from?

Every clip is a real microphone recording sourced from Wikimedia Commons under a permissive licence (CC0, Public Domain, CC BY, or CC BY-SA). The App ships 41 distinct cat sounds covering meows, purrs, hisses, growls, yowls, the occasional snore, eating, and even a cat-calling sound. Per-clip attribution is shown in the Credits screen.

How do I play a sound?

Open the Listen tab, tap any sound card. A compact mini-bar appears above the tab bar showing what is playing. Tap that mini-bar to open the Whisker Deck, where you can scrub, change loop mode, set a sleep timer, adjust pace and balance, and (with Pro) shape the tone with a 3-band EQ.

How do I layer two sounds?

Go to Blend. Pick a main clip and a bed clip — for example, a purr under a meow. Pro adds a third "Bed B" lane and lets you save your own named mixes for later recall.

How do I save a sound to my wishlist?

Tap the heart icon on any sound card. The Wishlist tab plays your hearted clips in order, shuffle, or — with Pro — smart-loop, cinematic, or bedtime auto-modes.

How do I turn a clip into a custom ringtone?

iOS does not allow apps to silently install custom ringtones from the sandbox. The App instead gives you a Clip Lab that:

  1. Lets you trim a sound to ≤ 30 seconds.
  2. Exports as M4R (ringtone format), AAC (.m4a), or WAV.
  3. Hands the file to the iOS share sheet so you can move it to Files, then drag it into GarageBand (or use the official Shortcuts ringtone flow) to install it as a custom ringtone.

Clip Lab export is a Pro feature.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open the App, go to the paywall or to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that originally bought the item. Restore re-checks your Apple receipt and unlocks any non-consumable purchases or active subscriptions.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us:

  1. Open Settings on your device.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Cat Sounds Music Ringtones and tap Cancel Subscription.

You will keep access until the end of the current billing period. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are issued by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase you want to report.

Why does the App ask for notifications permission?

Only if you set a sleep timer. iOS lets us post one optional alert when the timer ends, so playback does not just go silent in the background while you are doing something else. You can deny that permission and the sleep timer still works — it just fades the audio out without the notification.

Does the App use the microphone, camera, photos, or location?

No. The App ships its own audio library. It does not request microphone, camera, photos, contacts, or location permissions.

Why am I seeing ads, and can I remove them?

The free tier shows non-personalized ads served by Google AdMob. We do not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency and we do not share an IDFA. Upgrading to any Whisker Pro product — Weekly, Annual, or Forever — removes all advertising in the App.

I’m in the EU/UK and I want to change my ads consent

The consent prompt appears on first launch. You can revisit your choice from Settings → Ads consent in the App. Reinstalling the App also resets the consent prompt.

The App crashes or freezes — what should I try first?

  1. Force-quit and reopen. Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and swipe the App away, then reopen it.
  2. Restart the device. A simple restart clears most transient issues.
  3. Check for updates. Open the App Store, tap your profile, and update Cat Sounds Music Ringtones if a new version is available. Also update iOS in Settings → General → Software Update.
  4. Free up storage. Very low device storage causes apps to crash unexpectedly.
  5. Reinstall. Delete and reinstall the App from the App Store. Note: this also clears local data.
  6. Still failing? Email us at sandeepbhandari1@gmail.com with the details listed above.
