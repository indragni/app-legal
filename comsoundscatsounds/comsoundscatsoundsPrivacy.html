1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Cat Sounds Music Ringtones (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use the App on your iPhone or iPad. The App plays a curated library of real cat recordings, lets you layer them, save favourites, trim short slices for ringtones, and listen with a sleep timer. Almost everything happens on your device.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App and discontinue use.

2. Who we are

The App is published by the developer listed on its App Store page (the “Developer”). For privacy questions you can reach us at the email address in Section 15.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

The App does not require you to create an account or submit personal information (such as your name, email, address, or phone number) to use its core features. If a feature ever asks you to provide information — for example, to email us for support — it is optional and obvious.

3.2 Information processed automatically

The App itself does not use third-party analytics SDKs to track your behaviour, and we do not build a profile of you or your in-app activity. Apple separately shares standard App Store metrics (downloads, crash counts, anonymous engagement) with us via App Store Connect; we cannot identify individual users from those reports.

3.3 Device sensors and on-device processing

  • Motion (optional): if you enable the motion-based shuffle feature, iOS provides motion events to the App on-device only. The motion data never leaves your device.
  • Microphone: the App does not request microphone access. All audio shipped with the App is bundled.
  • Camera, Photos, Contacts, Location: not requested by this App.

4. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App’s features (playback, library, sleep timer, ringtone exports, widgets, App Intents).
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs (using the anonymous crash reports Apple provides through App Store Connect).
  • To respond to support requests you choose to send us.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. Advertising

The free tier of this App shows advertisements served through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). We have configured AdMob to serve non-personalized ads only: Google does not use your previous app activity or profile to choose which ads to show you. We do not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) to track you across other companies’ apps and websites, and we do not share an advertising identifier (IDFA) for cross-app tracking.

To serve and measure ads, AdMob may process limited information such as a device-resettable identifier, IP address, approximate (city-level) location, device model, OS version, and basic ad-interaction events (impression, click). This processing is performed by Google as a separate data controller under its own privacy terms.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App presents a Google-certified consent message (UMP) on first launch where you can accept or reject the use of cookies/identifiers for ads. You can change your choice at any time from the in-app settings or by reinstalling the App.

Upgrading to Whisker Pro (weekly, annual, or lifetime) removes all advertising in the App.

For more on Google’s data practices: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and AdMob & AdSense personalized advertising.

6. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

The App offers three optional Whisker Pro products, all processed entirely by Apple through the App Store:

  • Pro Weekly — auto-renewable, with a 3-day free trial introductory offer.
  • Pro Annual — auto-renewable.
  • Forever Pro — one-time, non-consumable.

Your payment details (such as card number) are handled by Apple — they are not visible to us. We receive only the receipt that confirms a purchase or subscription is active for your Apple ID, which the App uses on-device to unlock the corresponding features.

  • Subscriptions auto-renew until cancelled. You can manage or cancel at any time in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • You can restore previously purchased items from inside the App using the Restore Purchases button on the paywall or in Settings.

7. Children’s privacy

This App is rated 4+ on the App Store and is listed under a regular App Store category (Entertainment), not the Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. How information is shared

We share information only in these limited cases:

  • With Apple, as part of distributing the App through the App Store and processing in-app purchases.
  • With Google (AdMob), only if the App displays ads and only to the extent described in Section 5.
  • To comply with the law, if we are required to disclose information by a valid legal request.
  • In connection with a business transfer, where the App or its assets are transferred to another company. Any successor will be bound by terms at least as protective as this Policy.

9. Data retention & deletion

Because the App is designed to work without server-side accounts, the data it produces — your wishlist, recents, saved mixes, sleep-timer preferences, EQ settings, exported ringtone files — lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Removing the item from inside the App (toggle a heart off, delete a saved mix, clear a recent), or
  • Deleting the App from your device, which removes its local data.

Anonymous, aggregated reports we receive from Apple (downloads, crashes, etc.) are retained only for as long as needed to operate and improve the App.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding personal information we hold about you:

  • Access — confirm whether we hold personal information about you and request a copy.
  • Correction — ask us to correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete personal information we hold about you.
  • Restriction / objection — ask us to limit or stop certain processing.
  • Portability — request a copy in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on your consent (for example, ads consent), you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority (e.g., the ICO in the UK, your national DPA in the EU).

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what categories of personal information are collected and the right to opt out of any “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

To exercise any right, contact us at the email in Section 15. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information that we receive. Because the App stores nearly all of its data on your device, your device-level security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, encryption-at-rest provided by iOS) is the primary safeguard. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International data transfers

The App is offered worldwide through the App Store. The limited information processed by Apple and (if applicable) Google may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries where those companies operate. Apple and Google rely on appropriate transfer mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) for these transfers.

13. Bundled audio & images

Every cat recording shipped with the App is a real microphone recording sourced from Wikimedia Commons under one of the following licences: CC0, Public Domain, CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 2.5 / 3.0 / 4.0. Per-clip attribution lines are shown in the Credits screen inside the App and in the bundled ATTRIBUTION.md. Cat photographs that illustrate the sound cards are likewise sourced from Wikimedia Commons under the same licence family, with per-image attribution surfaced on the clip detail screen. No audio or image was generated or synthesised.
