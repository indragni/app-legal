Entertainment only. Face Age Scanner Detector renders a playful age band from Apple Vision geometry on your device. The reading is not medical, not biometric identity, and is not advice for hiring, insurance, or any other consequential decision.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Face Age Scanner Detector (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use the App on your iPhone or iPad. The App is built to work with the smallest possible amount of personal information, and the great majority of what it does happens entirely on your device.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App and discontinue use.

2. Who we are

The App is published by the developer listed on its App Store page (the “Developer”). For privacy questions you can reach us at the email address in Section 14.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

The App does not require you to create an account or submit personal information (such as your name, email, address, or phone number) to use its core features. If you optionally add a birthday for the local cross-check feature, that value is stored on your device only (in local SwiftData) and is never transmitted to us.

3.2 Information processed automatically

The App itself does not use third-party analytics SDKs to track your behaviour, and we do not collect a profile of you or your in-app activity for marketing. iOS will, separately and at the platform level, share standard App Store metrics (downloads, crash counts, anonymous engagement) with us via App Store Connect. Apple aggregates these and we cannot identify individual users from them.

3.3 Camera, photos, and on-device Vision processing

  • Camera: used only when you start a scan. A single still frame is captured for the Vision pass and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Photo library: used only for images you explicitly select to scan. The App reads the selected image, runs Apple Vision face detection and landmarks on your device, and discards the working data when you leave the screen unless you choose to save the readout to your local journal.
  • Vision pipeline: face rectangles, landmarks, and capture quality are computed locally with Apple’s on-device Vision framework. The estimated age “band” is a deterministic blend of those geometric signals — it is an entertainment readout, not a clinical or biometric measurement, and the source frames never leave the device.
  • No face embeddings or templates: we do not build, store, or transmit a biometric template that could be used to recognise you. The Vision data we use describes box dimensions and landmark positions for this one frame only.

3.4 Local journal & widgets

If you save a readout, the App stores a thumbnail and metadata (estimated age, expression cue, capture quality, timestamp) in local SwiftData on your device. Widgets, the Daily Lab Card, and Live Activities read this local data only. Nothing in the journal is uploaded.

4. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App’s features.
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs (using the anonymous crash reports Apple provides through App Store Connect).
  • To respond to support requests you choose to send us.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. Advertising

This app shows advertisements served through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). We have configured AdMob to serve non-personalized ads only: Google does not use your previous app activity or profile to choose which ads to show you. We do not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) to track you across other companies’ apps and websites, and we do not share an advertising identifier (IDFA) for cross-app tracking.

To serve and measure ads, AdMob may process limited information such as a device-resettable identifier, IP address, approximate (city-level) location, device model, OS version, and basic ad-interaction events (impression, click). This processing is performed by Google as a separate data controller under its own privacy terms.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app presents a Google-certified consent message (UMP) on first launch where you can accept or reject the use of cookies/identifiers for ads. You can change your choice at any time by reinstalling the App.

For more on Google’s data practices: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and AdMob & AdSense personalized advertising.

9. Data retention & deletion

Because the App is designed to work without server-side accounts, the data it produces (your saved readouts, journal, settings, birthday cross-check) lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Removing individual readouts from inside the App’s journal screen, or removing the birthday in Settings, or
  • Deleting the App from your device, which removes its local data.

Anonymous, aggregated reports we receive from Apple (downloads, crashes, etc.) are retained only for as long as needed to operate and improve the App.
