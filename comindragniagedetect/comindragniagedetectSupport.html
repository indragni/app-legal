Frequently asked questions

What is the “age band” actually showing?

The App reads a single image of a face with Apple’s on-device Vision framework, extracts geometric signals (bounding-box proportions, landmark positions, capture quality, smile openness, symmetry), and blends them into a deterministic “entertainment age band.” It is not a clinical measurement, not a biometric identity, and not advice for hiring, insurance, dating, or any other consequential decision. Different lighting, angle, or expression of the same person can produce different readouts — that is expected.

The reading seems off — what can I do?

Try a clearer, well-lit, straight-on portrait of just the face. Faces partially covered, in heavy shadow, looking sharply up or down, or behind glasses can confuse landmark detection and widen or skew the band. Switching on Lab precision pass in Settings (Pro) narrows the confidence window using extra landmark passes.

Does the app upload my photos or face data?

No. All Vision processing happens on your device. Camera frames and photo library selections are not transmitted to our servers, and we do not build or store a face template that could re-identify you. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open the App, go to the paywall, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that originally bought the item. Restore re-checks your Apple receipt and unlocks any non-consumable purchases (e.g. Forever Pro) or active subscriptions.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us:

  1. Open Settings on your device.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Face Age Scanner Detector and tap Cancel Subscription.

You will keep access until the end of the current billing period. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are issued by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase you want to report. We are happy to support a refund request, but only Apple can issue the refund.

The App is asking for permission to my Camera or Photos — why?

iOS requires us to ask the first time a feature needs that hardware. The App only asks for the permissions it actually uses, and only when the relevant feature is opened. You can review or change permissions any time in Settings → Face Age Scanner Detector. Denying a permission only disables the specific feature that needs it; the rest of the App keeps working.

How do I delete my data?

The App stores its journal, settings, and (if you set one) the birthday cross-check on your device. You can clear it by:

  • Removing individual readouts from the journal screen, or removing the birthday in Settings, or
  • Long-pressing the App icon on your Home Screen and choosing Remove App → Delete App. iOS will remove the App and its local data.

If you have specific privacy questions, see the Privacy Policy or email us at sandeep.bhandari@yahoo.co.in.

The App crashes or freezes — what should I try first?

  1. Force-quit and reopen. Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and swipe the App away, then reopen it.
  2. Restart the device. A simple restart clears most transient issues.
  3. Check for updates. Open the App Store, tap your profile, and update Face Age Scanner Detector if a new version is available. Also update iOS in Settings → General → Software Update.
  4. Free up storage. Very low device storage causes apps to crash unexpectedly.
  5. Reinstall. Delete and reinstall the App from the App Store. Note: this also clears local data.
  6. Still failing? Email us at sandeep.bhandari@yahoo.co.in with the details listed above.

Why am I seeing ads, and can I remove them?

Ads keep the free tier free. To remove ads, open the App and tap See plans & pricing in Settings → Pro — the Pro Weekly, Pro Annual, and one-time Forever Pro options all turn ads off. Ads we serve are non-personalized by default; we do not track you across other apps for advertising.
