1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Dot Switch: Color Tap Game (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use the App on your iPhone or iPad. We have designed the App to work with the minimum amount of personal information possible, and the great majority of what the App does happens entirely on your device.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App and discontinue use.

2. Who we are

The App is published by the developer listed on its App Store page (the “Developer”). For privacy questions you can reach us at the email address in Section 14.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

The App does not require you to create an account or submit personal information (such as your name, email, address, or phone number) to use its core features. If a feature ever asks you to provide information (for example, to email us for support), it will be obvious and entirely optional.

3.2 Information processed automatically

The App uses TelemetryDeck to collect a small set of anonymous product-interaction events (for example, when a run starts, when a palette is created, when a paywall is shown). TelemetryDeck is designed to be GDPR-friendly: events are aggregated and stored without any identifier that can be tied back to you personally, and we do not receive your IP address or device advertising identifier through it.

Crash and performance reports are collected on-device by Apple’s MetricKit framework and delivered to us in aggregated form by iOS. These reports contain technical information (stack traces, OS version, device model) and are used to diagnose stability and performance issues.

iOS will also, separately and at the platform level, share standard App Store metrics (downloads, crash counts, anonymous engagement) with us via App Store Connect. Apple aggregates these and we cannot identify individual users from them.

3.3 Game data stored on your device

Your game progress — best scores, custom palettes, unlocked skins and trails, daily streak, bonus-level progress, settings (haptics, theme, difficulty), and subscription/purchase receipts — is stored locally on your device using iOS’s standard UserDefaults store. None of this data is sent to a server we operate.

3.4 Device sensors and on-device processing

The App does not request access to device sensors (camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, HealthKit). It uses standard system features such as haptics, Live Activities, widgets, and notifications, all of which run on your device.

4. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App’s features (gameplay, palettes, bonus levels, daily challenge, subscriptions).
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs (using the anonymous crash reports Apple provides through App Store Connect and via MetricKit).
  • To understand which features are used and which to improve, using the anonymous TelemetryDeck events described in Section 3.2.
  • To respond to support requests you choose to send us.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. Advertising

This app shows advertisements served through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). We have configured AdMob to serve non-personalized ads only: Google does not use your previous app activity or profile to choose which ads to show you. We do not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) to track you across other companies’ apps and websites, and we do not share an advertising identifier (IDFA) for cross-app tracking.

To serve and measure ads, AdMob may process limited information such as a device-resettable identifier, IP address, approximate (city-level) location, device model, OS version, and basic ad-interaction events (impression, click). This processing is performed by Google as a separate data controller under its own privacy terms.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App presents a Google-certified consent message (UMP) on first launch where you can accept or reject the use of cookies/identifiers for ads. You can change your choice at any time by reinstalling the App.

Users who upgrade to the App’s Pro subscription or one-time lifetime purchase will not see ads, and the AdMob SDK is not asked to serve or measure ads for them.

For more on Google’s data practices: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and AdMob & AdSense personalized advertising.

6. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

The App offers optional in-app purchases and subscriptions (collectively, “Pro”) that unlock cosmetics, advanced power-ups, additional palette slots, and an ad-free experience. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Your payment details (such as card number) are handled by Apple — they are not visible to us. We receive only the receipt that confirms a purchase or subscription is active for your Apple ID, which we use to unlock the corresponding features.

  • Subscriptions auto-renew until cancelled. You can manage or cancel at any time in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • You can restore previously purchased non-consumable items from inside the App, in Settings → Restore Purchases.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is rated for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. How information is shared

We share information only in these limited cases:

  • With Apple, as part of distributing the App through the App Store and processing in-app purchases.
  • With Google (AdMob), only if the App displays ads to you, and only to the extent described in Section 5.
  • With TelemetryDeck, anonymous product-interaction events as described in Section 3.2.
  • To comply with the law, if we are required to disclose information by a valid legal request.
  • In connection with a business transfer, where the App or its assets are transferred to another company. Any successor will be bound by terms at least as protective as this Policy.

9. Data retention & deletion

Because the App is designed to work without server-side accounts, the data it produces (your saved scores, palettes, skins, settings, etc.) lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Removing the data from inside the App, where the feature exists, or
  • Deleting the App from your device, which removes its local data.

Anonymous, aggregated reports we receive from Apple (downloads, crashes) and from TelemetryDeck (product-interaction events) are retained only for as long as needed to operate and improve the App.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding personal information we hold about you:

  • Access — confirm whether we hold personal information about you and request a copy.
  • Correction — ask us to correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete personal information we hold about you.
  • Restriction / objection — ask us to limit or stop certain processing.
  • Portability — request a copy in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on your consent (for example, ads consent), you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority (e.g., the ICO in the UK, your national DPA in the EU).

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what categories of personal information are collected and the right to opt out of any “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

To exercise any right, contact us at the email in Section 14. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information that we receive. Because the App stores most of its data on your device, your device-level security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, encryption-at-rest provided by iOS) is the primary safeguard. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International data transfers

The App is offered worldwide through the App Store. The limited information processed by Apple, Google (AdMob), and TelemetryDeck may be transferred to and processed in the United States, the European Union, or other countries where those providers operate. Each provider relies on appropriate transfer mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) for these transfers.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the App, in third-party services, or in the law. We will revise the “Last updated” date below and, where the change is material, we will provide a more prominent notice in the App or on this page.
