Privacy Policy

Cartoon Sketch Avatar Photo is designed to work without creating an account.

Data we collect

We do not require you to submit personal information to use the core features.

On-device processing

When the app uses device sensors (for example, camera) it processes the data on your device for the requested feature.

Photos

If the app offers saving to Photos, it only saves items when you choose to save.

Face data

When you tap Suggest a Style, the app uses Apple's on-device Vision framework (VNDetectFaceRectanglesRequest) to check whether your photo contains a face. This is used only to pick a more suitable cartoon style preset (for example, a portrait-friendly look versus a landscape look). The app does not identify, recognize, verify, authenticate, or track any individual. It does not extract, generate, or store face embeddings, face geometry, landmarks, or biometric templates. The face-presence check runs entirely on your device, the result is a single boolean held in memory for the duration of the suggestion, and no face data is written to disk, included in saved presets or recent projects, transmitted off the device, shared with any third party, or retained after the suggestion is produced.
