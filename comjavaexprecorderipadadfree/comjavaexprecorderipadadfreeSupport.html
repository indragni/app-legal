Frequently asked questions

How do I start a new recording?

Open the App and tap the red Record button on the home screen. The Record screen opens and starts capturing as soon as the microphone is ready. You can pause and resume from the same screen, drop bookmarks while recording, and tap Save in the top-right when you’re done.

What is the “Bookmark moment” button for?

Tap it while recording to drop a marker at the current time. Bookmarks make it easy to jump back to important moments in long meetings, lectures, or interviews during playback.

Where are my recordings saved? Can I get them off the device?

Recordings live inside the App’s sandbox on your device, in M4A format. To export a copy, open a recording, tap the share icon, and use the system share sheet to send the file to Files, AirDrop, Mail, or any compatible app. Pro also lets you export as 16-bit / 44.1 kHz WAV.

How does transcription work? Does it upload my audio?

Transcription uses Apple’s on-device Speech framework. On supported devices, audio is converted to text locally — it is not uploaded to our servers or anyone else’s. Older devices that don’t support on-device recognition may fall back to Apple’s system transcription service; in that case Apple handles the audio under its own privacy terms.

Can I organize my recordings into folders?

Yes. The home screen lists default folders (Meetings, Lectures, Interviews, Notes) and you can rename, recolor, add, or delete folders from the folder icon in the top-left. Tap a folder to see only its recordings; long-press a recording in the list to move it.

How do I lock a recording so only I can open it?

With a Pro subscription, you can toggle Face ID / Touch ID protection on any individual recording from its detail screen. iOS handles the biometric match locally — we never receive your face or fingerprint data.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open the App, go to Settings → Pro, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that originally bought the item. Restore re-checks your Apple receipt and unlocks any non-consumable purchases or active subscriptions.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us:

  1. Open Settings on your device.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Voice Recorder Audio Notes and tap Cancel Subscription.

You will keep access until the end of the current billing period. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are issued by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase you want to report. We are happy to support a refund request, but only Apple can issue the refund.

The App is asking for permission to my Microphone, Speech Recognition, or Face ID — why?

iOS requires us to ask the first time a feature needs that capability. We only ask for the permissions the App actually uses, and only when the relevant feature is opened:

  • Microphone — required to record audio. Without it the App can’t record anything.
  • Speech Recognition — required to transcribe a recording into text. You can skip this and use the App as a recorder only.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — only used if you choose to lock a specific recording.

You can review or change permissions at any time in Settings → Voice Recorder Audio Notes. Denying a permission only disables the specific feature that needs it; the rest of the App keeps working.

How do I delete my recordings or all my data?

  • Delete one recording — swipe it in the library list and tap Delete, or long-press for the context menu’s Delete option.
  • Delete everything — long-press the App icon on your Home Screen and choose Remove App → Delete App. iOS removes the App and all its local data, including every recording and transcript.

If you have specific privacy questions, see the Privacy Policy or email us at sandeep.bhandari@yahoo.co.in.

A recording got cut off — what happened?

iOS automatically pauses recording when the audio route changes (for example, you unplug headphones, an incoming call interrupts, or another app grabs the microphone). The App also pauses if iOS sends an interruption notification. Your recording up to that point is preserved — open the Record screen and tap the play icon on the big red button to resume, then Save when you’re done.

The App crashes or freezes — what should I try first?

  1. Force-quit and reopen. Swipe up from the bottom of the screen (or double-press Home) and swipe the App away, then reopen it.
  2. Restart the device. A simple restart clears most transient issues.
  3. Check for updates. Open the App Store, tap your profile, and update Voice Recorder Audio Notes if a new version is available. Also update iOS in Settings → General → Software Update.
  4. Free up storage. Very low device storage causes apps to crash unexpectedly.
  5. Reinstall. Delete and reinstall the App from the App Store. Note: this also clears local data, including recordings.
  6. Still failing? Email us at sandeep.bhandari@yahoo.co.in with the details listed above.

Why am I seeing ads, and can I remove them?

The free version of the App is supported by Google AdMob ads. Subscribing to Pro (weekly with free trial, yearly, or lifetime) removes every ad. Ads are non-personalized by default — we do not track you across other apps for advertising.

I’m in the EU/UK and I want to change my ads consent

If a Google consent prompt appeared on first launch, you can revisit your choice from inside the App. Reinstalling the App also resets the consent prompt.
