1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Translate Voice Text Camera (“the App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use the App on your iPhone or iPad. The App is designed to translate text, voice, and camera input with the minimum amount of personal information possible — the great majority of what the App does happens entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Translation framework and (where available) Apple Intelligence.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App and discontinue use.

2. Who we are

The App is published by the developer listed on its App Store page (the “Developer”). For privacy questions you can reach us at the email address in Section 15.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

The App does not require you to create an account or submit personal information (such as your name, email, address, or phone number) to use its core features. If a feature ever asks you to provide information (for example, to email us for support), it will be obvious and entirely optional.

3.2 Information processed automatically

The App itself does not use third-party analytics SDKs that build a profile of you. We use TelemetryDeck, a privacy-preserving anonymous analytics service, to count usage events (such as “translation succeeded” or “phrase saved”); TelemetryDeck does not receive any identifier that lets us recognise you across apps or devices, and the events do not include the text you translate. iOS will, separately and at the platform level, share standard App Store metrics (downloads, crash counts, anonymous engagement) with us via App Store Connect — Apple aggregates these and we cannot identify individual users from them.

3.3 Content you translate

The text you type, the speech you dictate, and the images you capture or pick are processed on your device by Apple's on-device Translation, Speech, and Vision frameworks, and (when available) by Apple Intelligence. We do not send your translations to our servers. The App keeps a local history of recent translations and your saved phrases on your device only, using Apple's SwiftData / Core Data storage; you can delete any item from inside the App at any time.

3.4 No user tracking

The App does not implement Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt and does not link your activity in this App with data from other apps or websites for advertising purposes. We do not collect or use the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).

4. Device permissions

iOS requires us to ask before using certain hardware or system services. We only request the permissions a specific feature needs, and only when you open that feature. Denying a permission disables only that feature; the rest of the App keeps working.

  • Microphone — used on the Voice and Conversation tabs so the App can listen to what you say. Audio is processed on-device by Apple's Speech framework and is not uploaded.
  • Speech Recognition — used on the Voice and Conversation tabs to turn recorded audio into text for translation. Recognition happens on-device where the system supports it.
  • Camera — used on the Scan tab so you can snap a photo of foreign signs, menus, or product labels. On-device OCR reads the text and Apple's on-device Translation converts it into your chosen language. The captured image is not uploaded.
  • Photo Library — used on the Scan tab when you pick an existing photo to translate. OCR runs on-device using Apple's Vision framework.
  • Notifications — optional. Used only to confirm a Live Activity translation finished or to notify you of a saved-phrase reminder you opted into. We do not send marketing pushes.

5. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's features (translate, voice, scan, conversation, library).
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs (using anonymous crash reports Apple provides through App Store Connect and aggregate events from TelemetryDeck).
  • To respond to support requests you choose to send us.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

6. Advertising

The free version of the App displays ads through Google AdMob. Ads we serve are non-personalized by default — we do not pass any user identifier to AdMob for ad targeting, and AdMob is configured to request non-personalized ads (NPA) only.

  • In the EU, UK, Switzerland, and other regions where the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework applies, the App presents a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form on first launch so you can choose between personalized and non-personalized ads. You can revisit your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy → Ads consent inside the App.
  • Outside those regions, ads run as non-personalized.
  • AdMob receives only the technical information it needs to serve an ad (e.g., approximate region from IP, device type, ad-slot identifier). It does not receive your translations, voice recordings, photos, or any contact-style identifiers from us.
  • SKAdNetwork is used for privacy-preserving install attribution. This is Apple's anonymous attribution system and is not subject to the ATT prompt.
  • Pro subscribers and one-time Lifetime purchasers do not see ads.

For details on Google's data handling, see Google's advertising policy.

7. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

The App offers two auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time non-consumable purchase that unlock Pro features. Transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; your payment details are handled by Apple and are not visible to us. We receive only the receipt that confirms a purchase or subscription is active for your Apple ID, which we use to unlock the corresponding features.

  • Pro Weekly — auto-renews every week, with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers.
  • Pro Yearly — auto-renews every year.
  • Lifetime Pro — one-time purchase, no recurring charge.
  • Subscriptions auto-renew until cancelled. You can manage or cancel at any time in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • You can restore previously purchased non-consumable items from inside the App's paywall.

8. Children's privacy

The App is rated 4+ and is listed under a general App Store category — it is not part of Apple's Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. How information is shared

We share information only in these limited cases:

  • With Apple, as part of distributing the App through the App Store and processing in-app purchases.
  • With Google (AdMob), only to the extent described in Section 6, and only to serve non-personalized ads (unless you opt in to personalized ads in a region where that choice is offered).
  • With TelemetryDeck, for anonymous aggregate usage analytics that do not include translation content or any contact-style identifier.
  • To comply with the law, if we are required to disclose information by a valid legal request.
  • In connection with a business transfer, where the App or its assets are transferred to another company. Any successor will be bound by terms at least as protective as this Policy.

10. Data retention & deletion

Because the App is designed to work without server-side accounts, the data it produces (your saved phrases, recent translation history, settings) lives on your device. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Removing a specific item from inside the App (long-press a history row or phrase to delete it), or
  • Clearing all history from Settings → Reading → Clear history inside the App, or
  • Deleting the App from your device, which removes its local data.

Anonymous, aggregated reports we receive from Apple and TelemetryDeck are retained only for as long as needed to operate and improve the App.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights regarding personal information we hold about you:

  • Access — confirm whether we hold personal information about you and request a copy.
  • Correction — ask us to correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete personal information we hold about you.
  • Restriction / objection — ask us to limit or stop certain processing.
  • Portability — request a copy in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on your consent (for example, ads consent), you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint — with your local data protection authority (e.g., the ICO in the UK, your national DPA in the EU).

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know what categories of personal information are collected and the right to opt out of any “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

To exercise any right, contact us at the email in Section 15. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information that we receive. Because the App stores almost all of its data on your device, your device-level security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, encryption-at-rest provided by iOS) is the primary safeguard. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. International data transfers

The App is offered worldwide through the App Store. The limited information processed by Apple, Google, and TelemetryDeck may be transferred to and processed in the United States, the European Union, or other countries where those companies operate. They rely on appropriate transfer mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) for these transfers.
