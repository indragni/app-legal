Frequently asked questions

Does the App work offline?

Yes — once you've downloaded the language pair you want, translation runs entirely on-device. Open Settings → Offline & languages inside the App and tap Prepare on-device language packs to bring up Apple's pack-download sheet. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the App can also translate via Apple's on-device language model as a fallback when a language pack isn't available.

Why am I being asked to download a language?

Apple's on-device Translation framework downloads each language pair the first time you use it. After the pack is downloaded, that pair works offline. You can also pre-download packs from Settings → Offline & languages → Prepare on-device language packs.

Why does the Voice tab need the microphone and speech recognition?

The Voice and Conversation tabs listen to what you say and turn it into text for translation. iOS asks for both permissions the first time you tap the mic button. Audio is processed on-device by Apple's Speech framework and is not uploaded to our servers.

Why does the Scan tab ask for the camera and photo library?

The Scan tab can either take a new photo or pick an existing one from your library, then run on-device OCR (Apple's Vision framework) to read the text in the image. We don't upload the image; everything happens on your device.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open the App, open the paywall (tap Pro in the top-right of any main tab, or open Settings → Subscription), then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that originally bought the item. Restore re-checks your Apple receipt and unlocks any non-consumable purchases or active subscriptions.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us:

  1. Open Settings on your device.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Translate Voice Text Camera and tap Cancel Subscription.

You will keep access until the end of the current billing period. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are issued by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase you want to report. We are happy to support a refund request, but only Apple can issue the refund.

How do I delete my translation history or saved phrases?

The App stores history and saved phrases on your device only.

  • To delete a single item: open the Library tab, swipe a row to the left, and tap Delete.
  • To clear everything: open Settings → Reading inside the App and tap Clear history.
  • To wipe all local data: long-press the App icon on your Home Screen and choose Remove App → Delete App. iOS will remove the App and its local data.

For privacy questions, see the Privacy Policy or email us at sandeep.bhandari@yahoo.co.in.

The App crashes or freezes — what should I try first?

  1. Force-quit and reopen. Swipe up from the bottom of the screen (or double-press Home) and swipe the App away, then reopen it.
  2. Restart the device. A simple restart clears most transient issues.
  3. Check for updates. Open the App Store, tap your profile, and update Translate Voice Text Camera if a new version is available. Also update iOS in Settings → General → Software Update.
  4. Free up storage. Very low device storage causes apps to crash unexpectedly — especially when downloading language packs.
  5. Reinstall. Delete and reinstall the App from the App Store. Note: this also clears local data.
  6. Still failing? Email us at sandeep.bhandari@yahoo.co.in with the details listed above.

Why am I seeing ads, and can I remove them?

The free version is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. Ads we serve are non-personalized by default — we do not track you across other apps for advertising. Buying any Pro option (Weekly, Yearly, or Lifetime) removes ads immediately.

I'm in the EU/UK and I want to change my ads consent

If a consent prompt appeared on first launch, you can revisit your choice from Settings → Privacy → Ads consent inside the App. Reinstalling the App also resets the consent prompt.

The translation looks wrong — what now?

On-device translation models are excellent but not perfect, especially for slang, dialects, or technical jargon. Things you can try:

  • Tap Grammar polish on the source text first (Pro feature) — this rewrites your input for clarity before translating.
  • Tap the language swap arrow and translate back — the round-trip preview shows how close the meaning was preserved.
  • Split very long passages into shorter paragraphs.
  • Make sure you've selected the correct source language; the App's Detect button can help.
